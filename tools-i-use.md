Here's a list of -- mostly software development -- tools I enjoy using in my work and
elsewhere:

- WezTerm (tried: Kitty, Alacritty, and Warp back when it was just a terminal)
- SublimeText
- GitX

## Database viewers

- TablePlus
- Postico

These are about the same price, but TablePlus offers more features.
I just happened to purchase a license for Postico. It works very well.
Could I make the choice again I'd probably just purchase TablePlus.

## macOS Utilities

### Hammerspoon
Allows advanced actions on certain keypresses and keypresses.
All actions are coded in Lua.

I use this instead of BetterTouchTool.

### Karabiner
Allows advanced keyboard remaps. The ones I care most about are:

- Remap Caps Lock to Backspace.
- Remap right Shift to Escape.
